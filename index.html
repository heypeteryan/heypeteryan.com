About

I work on growth at Supabase, the Postgres development platform. I'm a two-time venture-backed founder based in Austin, Texas, and my career has centered on one problem: turning products people love into enterprise revenue.

From 2021 to 2025, I co-founded and ran HiFive as CEO. We took a go-to-network approach to go-to-market, building pipeline from warm intros and referrals. We raised $5.5M from PivotNorth, Hyperplane, SaaStr Fund, and others, grew to $5M ARR, and were acquired by HeyLois in November 2025. Before HiFive, I led the team selling Trello Enterprise and stayed on as an Enterprise Advocate at Atlassian after its $425M acquisition of Trello. I also co-founded Gogohire, a 500 Startups-backed hiring marketplace for top sales talent that grew to $200M GMV.

I started my career selling databases at Oracle, then joined LinkedIn as one of its first sales hires. I went from AE to Head of SMB Sales, started LinkedIn Sales Solutions, ran SMB sales for it, and landed its first 100 logos. In between, I ran business development at DoubleDutch. I'm a graduate of the University of Dayton.
